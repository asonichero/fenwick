Room editor

Its furniture to arrange: click a piece to choose it (a box is drawn round it) and drag it across the floor; the arrows nudge it as the camera sees the floor (Shift for five), Q and E turn it by 15° (Shift 5°), or type where it goes. Drag anywhere else to orbit; the wheel zooms. The walls nearest the camera are put out of the way. What stands on a piece (the feast on the table) goes with it. The walls, the hearth, the archway and the passage stay. Changes are kept in this browser as you make them, and the game builds the room from them.
